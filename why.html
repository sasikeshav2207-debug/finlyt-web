Four commitments we will not trade away

These are architecture decisions, not marketing positions, which is why they are hard to reverse later.

Your data never trains a model

Where AI generates commentary, the provider is contractually barred from retaining or training on the content. Every generation is consented and logged.

Read-only until you say otherwise

Every connector starts read-only. Nothing writes back into your books unless you explicitly enable it, and the audit log records it when you do.

One tenant, one company

Not row-level filtering on a shared table. Separate tenancy per company, which is the only version of this claim that survives a security review.

Leaving is as easy as arriving

Full export of everything, on request, in formats you can actually load elsewhere. No notice period and no retention clause.
