The Problem

flying blind on their financials

MIS takes too long to prepare

Month-end close, BRS, stock reconciliation, purchase matching — by the time MIS is ready, the moment to act has passed.

Investor meetings on the back foot

Building an Excel model the night before a pitch is not a strategy. It is a red flag investors notice immediately.

Most founders only discover a cash problem when it is already a crisis. Reactive is too late.

Western tools do not work here

Frequently Asked

Questions we hear
most often

If you don't see your question, write to founder@finlyt.net — usually replied within a day.

How does FinLytTech access my Tally data? +
Two ways. Either a one-time Tally Prime XML export — you upload, we generate. Or our read-only desktop Sync Agent that runs alongside your Tally on your office machine and pushes summary numbers (not transaction details) to your private FinLytTech account on a schedule. Both options keep your raw data inside your office; only the figures needed for your reports leave.
Is my financial data safe? +
Yes. Data is stored encrypted on India-hosted servers, isolated per company. We don't sell it, share it, or use it to train any AI model. You can request full deletion at any time. The full Privacy Policy is here.
Do I need to switch from Tally or Zoho? +
No. FinLytTech sits on top of your existing books. Your accountant continues working in Tally Prime or Zoho Books exactly as before. We read; we don't write.
How long does setup take? +
Under 5 minutes for the basic MIS — connect, upload, generate. For businesses that want segment-wise reporting (branches, projects, verticals), our CA-Assisted Implementation Programme takes about two weeks and gets your Tally configured properly with the Partner CA alongside.
Will my CA need to learn something new? +
No. Your CA keeps using Tally or Zoho. FinLytTech is for the people who consume the reports — founders, CFOs, investors. We are also actively partnering with CAs through the CA Partner Programme so they can offer FinLytTech under their own brand to all their clients at once.
Is there a free trial? +
Yes — 30 days, no credit card required. Connect your data and generate your first MIS report before paying anything. After the trial, plans start at ₹999/month + GST.
What if I'm a Chartered Accountant firm with many clients? +
The CA Partner Programme is built for you. One flat monthly arrangement gives you the platform across all your client books, white-labelled with your firm brand. You keep the client relationship; we provide the engine. Enquire in the For-CAs section above.
