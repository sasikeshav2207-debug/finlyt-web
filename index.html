Frequently Asked

Questions we hear
most often

If you don't see your question, — usually replied within a day.

How does FinLytTech access my Tally data? +
Two ways. Either a one-time Tally Prime XML export — you upload, we generate. Or our read-only desktop Sync Agent that runs alongside your Tally on your office machine and pushes summary numbers (not transaction details) to your private FinLytTech account on a schedule. Both options keep your raw data inside your office; only the figures needed for your reports leave.
Is my financial data safe? +
Yes. Data is stored encrypted on India-hosted servers, isolated per company. We don't sell it, share it, or use it to train any AI model. You can request full deletion at any time. The full Privacy Policy is here.
Do I need to switch from Tally or Zoho? +
No. FinLytTech sits on top of your existing books. Your accountant continues working in Tally Prime or Zoho Books exactly as before. We read; we don't write.
How long does setup take? +
Under 5 minutes for the basic MIS — connect, upload, generate. For businesses that want segment-wise reporting (branches, projects, verticals), our CA-Assisted Implementation Programme takes about two weeks and gets your Tally configured properly with the Partner CA alongside.
Will my CA need to learn something new? +
No. Your CA keeps using Tally or Zoho. FinLytTech is for the people who consume the reports — founders, CFOs, investors. We are also actively partnering with CAs through the CA Partner Programme so they can offer FinLytTech under their own brand to all their clients at once.
Is there a free trial? +
Yes — 30 days of MIS Essentials (₹999/month tier), no credit card required. Connect your data and generate your first MIS report before paying anything. After the trial, MIS Essentials continues at ₹999/month + GST.

Operations Intelligence (₹3,999/mo) and AI Cash Flow Forecasting (₹5,999/mo) require a paid subscription from day 1 — you can preview what each tier delivers on the Live Demo, then activate directly. Mid-trial upgrades from Essentials are smooth — switch any time and the new tier kicks in immediately.
How do AI credits work? +
Every plan includes a fair monthly allowance of AI credits sized for typical use of that tier. One credit covers one AI-generated narrative, one AI-drafted debtor / creditor follow-up email, or one scenario analysis run. Viewing existing output, browsing the dashboard, downloading PDFs / Excel and deterministic forecasts & predictive alerts use zero credits — that's computation, not generation. If you ever exceed your allowance, you can buy overage packs at tier-appropriate per-credit rates shown clearly inside the dashboard before purchase. Unused credits don't roll over. No silent overcharges — hard cap with explicit opt-in, by design.
Why do you ask me to confirm before each AI generation? +
Consent-by-design. Every AI generation shows a one-time confirmation — "This will use 1 credit. Continue?" — so you stay in control. You can opt to skip this prompt for the rest of your session if you prefer fewer interruptions. There is no auto-regeneration: AI output refreshes only when you click Regenerate, or naturally when you load new books / press F5.
Where is my data processed for AI features? +
AI narratives are generated using Anthropic's Claude API over an encrypted connection. Only aggregated MIS figures are sent — no transaction-level personal data, no individual voucher detail, no customer / vendor PII. Anthropic does not retain or train on your data per their API terms. Full disclosure in our Privacy Policy.
What if I'm a Chartered Accountant firm with many clients? +
The CA Partner Programme is built for you. One flat monthly arrangement gives you the platform across all your client books, white-labelled with your firm brand. You keep the client relationship; we provide the engine. Enquire in the For-CAs section above.
