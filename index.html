The questions that actually get asked.

How does FinLytTech access my accounting data?+

A small TDL file inside Tally, standard APIs for Zoho and ERPNext, a generic REST adapter for anything else, and every connector runs read-only unless you opt in.

Is my financial data safe?+

Encrypted in transit and at rest on India-hosted servers, never used to train third-party models, and deletable on request.

Do I need to switch from my current accounting system?+

No, it sits alongside whatever you already run, and moving to the FinLytTech ERP later is a load, not a re-implementation.

How long does setup take?+

A day for MIS Essentials, two weeks with CA-Assisted Implementation if the books need cleaning first, six to eight weeks for the ERP.

Is there a free trial?+

Yes, thirty days free on MIS Essentials with no card, and an extra month for early sign-ups.
