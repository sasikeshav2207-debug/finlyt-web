The questions that actually get asked.

More on the FAQ page.

How does FinLytTech access my accounting data?+

A small TDL file inside Tally, standard APIs for Zoho and ERPNext, a generic REST adapter for any other ERP, or an Excel drop zone. Every connector runs read-only unless you opt in.

How is it priced?+

Per named login, per month: Cashflow Analytics ₹1,000, Business Decisions ₹5,000, Capital Access ₹10,000. The Enterprise ERP is by conversation and the Custom Enterprise Dashboard by quotation. See pricing

Is my financial data safe?+

Encrypted in transit and at rest on India-hosted servers, never used to train third-party models, and deletable on request.

Do I need to switch from my current accounting system?+

No. The Solutions sit on whatever you already run, and moving to the FinLytTech ERP later is a load of the same journal, with nothing to migrate.

Does Capital Access give investment advice?+

No. It identifies cash above your working-capital need and lets you connect with advisors, distributors and wealth managers in a neutral directory, with no ranking, no yields and no return claims. You deal with them directly.

What if I'm a Chartered Accountant firm with many clients?+

The firm buys seats for the people who build client packs, and the CA Partner Programme adds white-label deployments and revenue share on referred clients. For CAs
