What does your team stop doing by hand?

The judgement stays with your team. The rebuilding goes to the platform, and every figure it produces traces back to a posted voucher your staff can open.

Rebuilding the monthly pack

The workbook your staff reconstruct from exported ledgers each month is generated from the client's own books instead, in the Schedule III order.

Chasing ledgers for positions

Receivables, payables and statutory dues are read from the books and placed on the Finance Calendar on the day they land.

Answering "where did the cash go?"

Clients log in and see it for themselves. The call they make to your office is about what to do next.

How does CA-Assisted Implementation run?

A guided onboarding plan for your first cohort of clients. We work through it with your team so the pattern is set before you run it alone. The pace depends on the state of each client's books.

Step 01
Connect

The TDL file goes into Tally, or the API keys go in for Zoho and ERPNext. Read-only, then the first sync runs.

Step 02
Clean

Group the ledgers, fix the mapping, agree the Schedule III hierarchy. This is the part that actually takes the time.

Step 03
Brand

Your logo, palette and firm details across dashboards and exports, so what the client opens carries your name.

Step 04
Hand over

Your team runs the next client with us watching rather than driving. After that the cohort is yours.

What do partner firms ask first?

Does this replace what my team does?+

It takes over the rebuilding. The workbook your team reconstructs every month gets generated from the books, and the judgement on what it means stays with your team. That frees the hours for advisory work clients value.

How is a firm billed?+

Per named login, per month, at the Solution that login uses: ₹1,000 for Cashflow Analytics, ₹5,000 for Business Decisions, ₹10,000 for Capital Access. See seat pricing for the detail.

Whose brand does the client see?+

Yours. On partner deployments the dashboard and the exports carry your firm's identity. FinLytTech stays underneath it.

What if a client's books are a mess?+

Then the cleaning is the work, and that is exactly what CA-Assisted Implementation covers. We do it with you on the first cohort so the pattern is established before you scale it.

Is client data isolated between tenants?+

Single-tenant per company, always. Switching between client books in one console never means their data shares a table.
