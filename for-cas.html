What partner firms ask first

Does this replace what my team does?+

It replaces the rebuilding, not the judgement. The workbook your team reconstructs every month gets generated instead, which frees the hours for advisory work clients will actually pay a premium for.

Yours. On partner deployments the dashboard, the exports and the emailed board pack all carry your firm's identity. We stay underneath it.
