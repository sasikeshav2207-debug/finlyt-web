9.1 AI Credits

AI features that generate output (narratives, AI-drafted debtor / creditor follow-up emails, scenario analysis runs, loan-readiness CMA generation) consume AI credits. Each subscription tier includes a fixed monthly allowance of credits, sized for typical usage of that tier. The exact monthly allowance, per-credit overage price, and current balance are shown inside your dashboard at all times and confirmed at the point of every generation.

  • Free actions (no credits): viewing existing AI output, browsing the dashboard, downloading PDF / Excel exports, deterministic forecasts and predictive alerts. These are computation, not generation.
  • No rollover: unused credits expire at month-end and do not carry forward.
  • No auto-regeneration: existing AI output never refreshes by itself. It is regenerated only when (a) you explicitly click Regenerate, or (b) you load new books / press F5 to refresh.
  • Consent on every generation: the system displays "This will use 1 credit. Continue?" before each AI run. You may opt to skip this prompt for the rest of your session.
  • Hard cap with explicit opt-in for overage: when your monthly allowance is exhausted, AI-generate buttons are disabled. You may purchase an overage pack to continue — you must explicitly opt in to each purchase. We do not silently charge you on overage. Existing AI output remains visible regardless.
  • Trial: the free trial includes a small sampler of credits, displayed inside the dashboard, sufficient to evaluate AI quality end-to-end. On conversion to a paid plan your tier's monthly allowance applies.
  • Overage packs are billed at the per-credit price displayed in the dashboard at the time of purchase. Prices are tier-dependent and may be revised on 30 days' notice; existing prepaid packs are honoured at the rate paid.
  • Refunds: unused overage credits are non-refundable once purchased, except where required by law. We do not refund the bundled monthly allowance.

No false promises. AI credits are a real cost to FinLytTech (we pay our model provider per call). The credit system exists to keep the product economics sustainable and the pricing transparent to you. We do not promise unlimited AI generation on any plan.
