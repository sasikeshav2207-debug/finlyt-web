What's included

Six structured workstreams,
delivered together

Each engagement is scoped to your business. The six workstreams below are the standard programme — your Partner CA tailors depth to your scale and history.

01

Ledger architecture

Clean grouping, proper Sundry Debtors / Creditors classification, expense buckets — so every report rolls up correctly from the first sync.

02

Cost-centre framework

Tally's cost-centre layer configured for your business shape — branches, projects, verticals — so segment P&L works the moment you sync.

03

Bill-wise outstanding setup

Receivables and payables anchored to invoice references — so ageing buckets, debtor days and concentration metrics reflect reality, not noise.

04

GSTIN tagging & reconciliation

Every party tagged with its GSTIN, every voucher with the right rate — so GST exposure and ITC are dashboard-ready, not month-end fire drills.

05

FinLytTech onboarding

TDL paste, agent key, first sync, dashboard walkthrough — your team is on the platform and reading reports inside week one.

06

Two-week guided support

Your Partner CA stays alongside through your first close — every voucher entered correctly, every report verified, until your team runs it without help.

Who delivers it

empanelled CA partners

Qualified Chartered Accountants, trained on the FinLytTech platform — and only on the FinLytTech platform — so the configuration is built specifically for how your dashboards will read it.

Two clean relationships, one outcome

Your service contract for the implementation work sits with the Partner CA firm — an independent practice empanelled with FinLytTech. Your FinLytTech subscription sits separately with us, for the software.

This structure keeps the accountancy engagement squarely within the framework set out by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, and gives you the assurance that a qualified professional — not a software vendor — is responsible for how your books are configured.

The outcome: a Tally configured to surface every insight your business needs, and a dashboard that reads it correctly from day one.

After implementation, your FinLytTech subscription starts at MIS Essentials — ₹999/month + GST, with Fin Ops Intelligence (₹3,999) and AI Cash Flow Forecasting (₹5,999) available when your needs grow.
