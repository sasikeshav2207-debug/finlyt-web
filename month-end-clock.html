The Month-End Clock

How many days does your month-end pack eat?

Tell us who builds the monthly cash and MIS pack, what they earn and how long it takes. We will show what that time is worth, which days come back, and where your team could spend them instead.

Capacity, never headcount

Nobody in this picture is replaced. The rupee figure is the value of senior finance time spent assembling numbers, which the same people can put back into collections, planning and the conversations only they can have.

Which days come back, and why do the others stay?

The days on the pack
Pulling, reconciling, ageing, rebuilding the sheet.

This is assembly. FinLytTech reads the books you already keep, Tally, Zoho, ERPNext, any ERP via API or an Excel drop zone, and does it from the ledger itself.

Review and sign-off
Judgement stays with your team.

The clock always leaves the review day with the most senior person on the pack. Switch on "every role reviews" in the settings if your process asks for more.
