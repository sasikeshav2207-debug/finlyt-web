Numbers are easy. **Insight** is the hard part.

If you run a business in India — or you're the CA who supports one — you already know the gap. Your books are full of truthful, accurate data. But finding the *answer* — what's actually changing this month, where the cash is going, which customer is quietly slipping — takes hours of pivot tables and stitched-together spreadsheets.

Finlyt was built to close that gap. We turn the financial data you already have (in Tally, Zoho Books, or anywhere else) into the **investor-grade MIS, cash-flow forecast and real-time insight** that growing businesses need — without rebuilding the way you keep your books.

## What you'll find here

This blog is where we'll think out loud. Expect three kinds of posts:

- **Product notes** — what's new in Finlyt, why we built it that way, and how to get the most out of it.
- **MIS deep-dives** — practical pieces on financial reporting that go beyond the obvious. Ratios that actually move decisions. The right way to read a P&L when inventory is integrated. Why a cash-flow statement that doesn't reconcile is silently lying to you.
- **Field lessons** — the messy realities of working with Indian SME books: on-account debtors, contra accounts, integrated inventory, FY transitions. We'll share what we learn as we work with more companies.

## Why a blog, and why now

Two reasons.

One — **the questions are bigger than a LinkedIn post**. A lot of what we want to share doesn't fit a 1,300-character update. A blog gives us room.

Two — **we want it to be findable**. When someone six months from now searches "how to read a Tally trial balance when inventory is integrated," we want the honest, useful answer to be ours.

We'll also re-publish the longer LinkedIn pieces here, lightly tightened, so everything lives in one searchable place.
