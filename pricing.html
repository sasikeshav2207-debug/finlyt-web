Questions that come up about price

Is it really per company, not per seat?+

Yes. Add your whole finance team, your founders and your chartered accountant on any plan without the price moving. Charging per seat would push exactly the wrong behaviour, which is fewer people looking at the numbers.

What happens after the 30 days?+

Nothing automatic. There is no card on file, so the trial simply ends and you decide whether to continue. Early sign-ups get a second month on us.

Can I move between tiers?+

Up or down, at any time, and the change applies from the next billing month. The underlying data does not move, so nothing is rebuilt when you switch.

Do chartered accountants get different terms?+

Yes. The CA Partner Programme carries white-label deployments, revenue share on referred clients and tiered economics that improve as your book grows.

Start on the cheapest tier. Move up only when it stops fitting.
