Questions that come up about price

What counts as a login?+

One named person who signs in. Each login is priced at the Solution it carries.

How do I try it before paying?+

Start with a demo on your own books. Bring last month's ledger and we walk through what each Solution shows on it.

Can a login move to a different Solution?+

Yes. All three Solutions read the same substrate, so the data underneath does not move and nothing is rebuilt when a login changes.

How do chartered accountants buy?+

CA firms buy seats inside the firm, at the same per-login prices. The seat model and the CA Partner Programme are set out on the page for CA firms.
