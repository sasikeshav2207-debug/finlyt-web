Delivering Excellence through FinTelligence

From the first invoice to the IPO prospectus.

That is a large claim, so here is what stands behind it. Six phases on one journal — the same posted vouchers a ten-person company starts with are the ones a merchant banker reads during DRHP prep.

  • 01 MIS Essentials
  • 02 Fin Ops Intelligence
  • 03 AI Cash Flow Forecasting
  • 04 Capital Suite
  • 05 Consolidation & Ops Intelligence
  • 06 IPO Readiness

Sequence, not a delivery schedule · each phase ships when the one before it is stable

  1. 01

    MIS Essentials

    Live T1 · ₹999/mo

    Your books, board-ready. Connect Tally, Zoho Books, ERPNext or any ERP with an API, and the statements assemble themselves — no re-keying, no month-end spreadsheet relay.

    • P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and trial balance, Schedule III presentation
    • Ratios, ageing and customer concentration off the same posted data
    • Comparative periods, so a number is always read against something
    • Export to the formats a board pack and a lender actually accept
  2. 02

    Fin Ops Intelligence

    Shipping T2 · ₹3,999/mo

    Where reporting stops and operating begins. The statements tell you what happened; this tier tells you what to do on Monday.

    • Working capital and the cash conversion cycle, tracked rather than computed once a year
    • Customer concentration and vendor analytics — who you actually depend on
    • Product and customer margin, so the loss-making line is visible before the year end
    • MSME exposure under the 45-day rule, computed from posted vouchers
  3. 03

    AI Cash Flow Forecasting

    In build T3 · ₹5,999/mo

    A thirteen-week rolling view of the only number that closes a business. The forecasting engine already runs inside the ERP layer; this phase brings it into the MIS product with the interface a finance lead can actually argue with.

    • Thirteen-week rolling forecast with confidence bands, not a single false line
    • Scenario planning — change an assumption, see the runway move
    • Cash-runway alerts before the position becomes the problem
    • Model comparison, so a forecast can be checked against what happened
  4. 04

    Capital Suite

    Planned T3+

    Raising money is a document problem long before it is a pitch problem. This phase turns the journal you already keep into the pack a lender or an investor expects.

    • Loan-readiness score, computed from your own books rather than a form
    • CMA generator in the formats banks actually accept
    • Lender-grade report layouts, signed off against CA practice
    • Lender and investor matchmaking
  5. 05

    Consolidation & Operations Intelligence

    Planned

    The point at which a group stops being several companies in several systems, and the point at which finance data stops being the only data.

    • Multi-company consolidation — holding, subsidiaries and associates, with proper elimination entries
    • CRM, WMS and HRMS data in alongside the ledger
    • Findings your accounting system alone cannot reach — a top-margin customer whose order rate has quietly halved
    • The ERP layer and the MIS line converge into one platform
  6. 06

    IPO Readiness

    Planned

    Everything a scaling company needs to file a DRHP and list — not bolted on at the end by a different vendor, but the same platform, holding the same journal, that it started on.

    • Ind AS parallel books — every voucher fans out to IGAAP and Ind AS through the multi-book engine
    • Full XBRL tagging in the MCA taxonomy
    • An auto-populated DRHP data room: financials, related-party matrix, board minutes, litigation, IP and lease registers
    • Related-party auto-flagging against the financial graph
    • The prospectus number pack — the restated financials, ratios and MD&A a merchant banker asks for, each traceable to a posted voucher
    • Continuous audit trail, auditor collaboration login, and SEBI LODR filings

Why one journal matters

Most companies change systems three or four times on the way up, and every change costs the history. The restatement work at DRHP stage is largely archaeology — reconstructing years of decisions from systems nobody runs any more.

A single continuous journal removes that work, because the audit trail was never broken in the first place.
