Why does one substrate matter?

Most businesses change systems several times on the way up, and every change costs the history. Reconstructing old decisions out of systems nobody runs any more is slow, expensive work.

FinLytTech keeps one substrate underneath all three lines. The Solutions are a query over the books you already keep. The Enterprise ERP is the same query against a database you control, and a Custom Enterprise Dashboard is that database shaped to your industry. There is nothing to migrate between them, because there is nothing to migrate.
