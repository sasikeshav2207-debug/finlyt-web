What it is

What is FinLytTech?+

A management decision-making platform for Indian businesses. It reads the books you already keep, finds the money they have lost track of, and says how to get it back. If you cannot say where your cash went, who owes you, who you owe, how long you survive and what is due this week, it is built for you, whatever the size of the business.

What are the three Solutions?+

Cashflow Analytics shows where cash came from, where it went, who owes you, who you owe and how long the business survives. Business Decisions adds P&L analytics, customer concentration, vendor analytics and an eight-week cash projection, shown as a risk position that moves from red to green with the action items attached. Capital Access connects you to credit, insurance and, when there is spare cash, to advisors you deal with directly.

Where does each number come from?+

From a posted voucher in your own books. The platform computes every figure, the model writes the sentence around it, and any sentence carrying a figure the platform did not compute is discarded before you see it.

Connecting your books

How does FinLytTech access my accounting data?+

A small TDL file inside Tally, standard APIs for Zoho and ERPNext, and a generic REST adapter for anything else. An Excel drop zone covers books kept in spreadsheets. Every connector runs read-only unless you explicitly opt in to write-back.

My system is not on your list. Now what?+

If it speaks REST, the API adapter handles it. That is the route for SAP, NetSuite, Dynamics, Busy, Marg, Sage, QuickBooks and Xero. Map the fields once and the journal fills itself from then on. If it only exports files, the Excel drop zone reads those instead. See every source.

Security & data

Is my financial data safe?+

Encrypted in transit and at rest on India-hosted servers, built to the DPDP Act from the start, and deletable on request. Each company sits in its own tenant. Nothing is shared through a filter on a common table.

What exactly does the AI see?+

Only what is needed to write the commentary, only with your explicit consent, and the provider is contractually barred from training on it. Every generation is logged against the user who triggered it.

Setup & switching

Do I need to switch from my current accounting system?+

No. The Solutions sit on top of whatever you already run. If you later move to the FinLytTech ERP, that move is a load onto the same substrate. There is nothing to migrate between the Solutions, the ERP and a custom deployment.

How does setup work?+

For the Solutions, connect the source and let the first sync run. If the ledgers need grouping and mapping first, CA-Assisted Implementation covers that with your accountant. The ERP runs pilot-first over six to eight weeks. A Enterprise Intelligence is scoped and quoted per engagement.

Pricing

How is it priced?+

Per named user login, per month. Cashflow Analytics is ₹1,000, Business Decisions ₹5,000 and Capital Access ₹10,000. The ERP and Enterprise Intelligence are quoted. See pricing for the full comparison.

How do I try it?+

Start with a demo on your own books. Book thirty minutes with the founding team and bring a trial balance.

For CA firms

How does a chartered accountant firm with many clients use it?+

The firm buys seats inside the practice, one per named login, and works across every client book from one console. Your staff stop rebuilding the monthly pack by hand, and your clients log in to see their own position.

What is the CA Partner Programme?+

The Partner Programme puts your firm's identity on client-facing dashboards, shares subscription revenue on clients you bring, and includes guided onboarding for your first cohort.

Capital Access & health score

Does FinLytTech give investment advice?+

No. When the platform finds cash sitting above your working-capital need, Capital Access lets you connect with investment advisors, mutual fund distributors, asset managers and wealth managers and deal with them directly. It is a neutral directory, with no ranking, no yields and no return or performance claims. The advice comes from the advisor you choose.

What is the financial health score? In build+

A score shaped like a credit score: a black box you cannot game, which still shows what raises it and what lowers it. It is in build and not yet available.
