Connecting your books

How does FinLytTech access my accounting data?+

A small TDL file inside Tally, standard APIs for Zoho and ERPNext, and a generic REST adapter for anything else. Every connector runs read-only unless you explicitly opt in to write-back.

Do I need to switch from my current accounting system?+

No. It sits alongside whatever you already run, and moving to the FinLytTech ERP later is a load rather than a re-implementation.

My system is not on your list. Now what?+

If it speaks REST, the generic adapter handles it. Map the fields once and the journal fills itself from that night onwards. If it only exports files, we can read those on a schedule instead.

Security & data

Is my financial data safe?+

Encrypted in transit and at rest on India-hosted servers, never used to train third-party models, and deletable on request. Each company sits in its own tenant rather than behind a filter on a shared table.

What exactly does the AI see?+

Only what is needed to write commentary on a variance you asked about, only when you ask, and the provider is contractually barred from retaining or training on it. Every generation is logged against the user who triggered it.

Setup & migration

How long does setup take?+

A day for MIS Essentials. Two weeks with CA-Assisted Implementation if the books need cleaning first. Six to eight weeks for the ERP, pilot-first.

What happens to my historical data?+

Two years of history comes across in the first sync, which is what makes the forecast meaningful from day one rather than after a year of accumulation. Longer histories are a migration template away.

Trial & commercials

Is there a free trial?+

Thirty days free on MIS Essentials with no card, and an extra month for early sign-ups. Nothing charges automatically at the end because there is no card on file.

Is pricing per user?+

Per company. Add your whole team and your CA without the price moving. See pricing for the full comparison.

For CA firms

What if I am a chartered accountant firm with many clients?+

The CA Partner Programme gives you white-label deployments, revenue share on referred clients and two-week onboarding for your first cohort.
