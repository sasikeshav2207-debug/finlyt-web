4 Your Financial Data — Special Section

This is the most important section for business users. Your company's financial data — P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, ledger entries — is never stored on FinLytTech's servers.

When you request a report, FinLytTech:

  1. Uses your stored OAuth token to make a secure API call to Zoho Books (or parses your uploaded Tally XML)
  2. Processes the data in memory on our server to calculate ratios, variances, and generate AI narrative
  3. Returns the report to your browser
  4. Discards the raw financial data from server memory

For Tally XML uploads: the uploaded XML file is processed and immediately deleted from our servers. It is not retained.

The AI narrative is generated by Anthropic's Claude API. Financial data is sent to Anthropic's API for this purpose under their data processing agreement. Anthropic does not use your data to train models (see their privacy policy at anthropic.com).

7 Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the following rights as a Data Principal:

  • Right to access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to grievance redressal — raise a complaint with us and receive a response within 30 days

To exercise any of these rights, email us at founder@finlyt.net with the subject line "Data Rights Request". We will respond within 30 days.

Account deletion: To delete your FinLytTech account and all associated data, email founder@finlyt.net. We will delete your account within 7 business days. This will revoke your Zoho Books connection and delete all stored tokens.

9 Service Telemetry and Error Logging

To operate the FinLytTech platform reliably and to diagnose issues you may encounter, we automatically record a limited set of service-operation events when you use the platform. We rely on legitimate interest as our legal basis under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we record:

  • Sync events between your accounting software and the dashboard (success, failure, error class)
  • Report generation events (PDF / Excel exports)
  • Connection events (Tally pairing, Zoho authorization)
  • Authentication events (sign-in, sign-out, password reset, idle timeout, two-factor enablement)
  • Application errors encountered in your browser or on our servers

What we deliberately do NOT record:

  • Your financial data (P&L figures, balances, transaction details)
  • Your customers' or vendors' names or amounts
  • The contents of any file you upload
  • Mouse movements, scrolling behaviour, or click-stream
  • Page-view analytics

Each record contains: the event name, a sanitised metadata payload, your account identifier, your IP address, and your browser identifier. The payload is automatically scrubbed of any pattern that resembles a JWT token, API key, PAN, GSTIN, or Aadhaar number, and any string longer than 500 characters is truncated.

Retention: These records are stored for 90 days and then automatically purged.

How we use these records:

  • Diagnose and resolve errors you report to support
  • Identify systemic issues affecting multiple customers
  • Demonstrate the integrity of the platform if asked by a regulator or auditor

We do not use these records for marketing, profiling, ad targeting, or any purpose other than the ones listed above. We do not share these records with third parties.

Your rights specific to this section:

  • You may request a copy of your telemetry records at any time by writing to founder@finlyt.net, or by signing in and pulling them yourself from the dashboard.
  • You may request deletion of your telemetry records at any time (we will action this within 7 working days). This does not affect your right to continue using the service.

These rights are in addition to the data subject rights set out in Section 7 above.

10 Support Tickets and Communications

When you open a support ticket from inside the dashboard (the green "?" button bottom-right of every page), we process and store the information needed to track, resolve, and follow up on your request. Our legal basis is contractual necessity under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — the support relationship is part of the service you've signed up for.

What we collect when you open a ticket:

  • The title and description you write
  • The category you select (Sync issue / Data accuracy / Account / Feature request / Other)
  • The severity you select (Low / Normal / High)
  • Optional page context — the page URL you were on and your browser identifier. You see this in a preview before submitting and can uncheck the box to omit it.
  • The email address tied to your account, so we can reply

What we deliberately do NOT collect on a ticket:

  • Screenshots (this is a v1 limitation by design — when we add screenshot upload, we will update this section first)
  • Form-field contents from other pages
  • Financial data from your dashboard, reports, or Tally export files
  • Any data outside the title, description, category, severity, and the optional page-context block

How we use ticket data:

  • To respond to your specific request and resolve the issue
  • To trace the issue if it affects other customers (we may reference the situation in internal notes without disclosing your identity to other customers)
  • To inform improvements to the platform

Internal notes: Our support team may add internal notes against your ticket that you do not see (for example, technical investigation notes). These never leave the ticket record and are visible only to FinLytTech operators.

Email notifications: When you open a ticket, when our team replies, and when you reply back, we may send email notifications using the Zoho Mail service to your account email address. You can stop receiving these by closing the ticket (the "Resolved" state stops further notifications).

Retention:

  • Tickets in the Resolved or Closed state are retained for 2 years from the date of resolution, then deleted, in line with our general account-data retention policy.
  • Tickets in Open states (New, In Progress, Awaiting Customer Reply) are retained until they are resolved or closed.
  • If you delete your account, all your tickets are deleted as part of the cascade. You can request retention of a specific ticket for your records by writing to founder@finlyt.net before requesting account deletion.

We do not share ticket data with third parties, and we do not use the contents of your tickets for marketing, profiling, or model training of any kind.

Your rights specific to support tickets:

  • You may view every ticket you've opened, and the full conversation on each, at any time at dashboard.finlyt.net/tickets.
  • You may request export or deletion of your support history at any time by writing to founder@finlyt.net (action within 7 working days).
  • You may correct factual inaccuracies in a ticket you've opened by replying on the thread with the correction.

11 Security Measures

  • All data transmitted over TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS everywhere)
  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt — never stored in plain text
  • OAuth tokens encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • API keys stored as environment variables — never in code
  • GitHub repository does not contain any secrets or credentials
  • Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) — users can only access their own data
  • Regular dependency updates and security patches

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as required under the DPDP Act 2023.
